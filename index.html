♔ King

The most important piece. Moves one square in any direction. The goal is to checkmate the opponent's King. Keep it safe!

♕ Queen

The most powerful piece. Moves any number of squares in any direction. Losing it early is usually very bad!

♖ Rook

Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically. Strongest on open files. Connect your Rooks early!

♗ Bishop

Moves diagonally and stays on its starting color forever. Two Bishops covering both colors are very powerful.

♘ Knight

Moves in an L-shape and can jump over pieces! Strongest in the center of the board.

♙ Pawn

Moves forward one square (two from start). Captures diagonally. Reaches the other end and promotes — usually to a Queen!

🏁 Objective

Checkmate your opponent's King — put it under attack with no escape.

⚡ Check

When a King is under attack it is in check. You must escape immediately — move the King, block, or capture the attacker.

🤝 Stalemate

If a player has no legal moves but the King is NOT in check, the game is a draw.

👑 Promotion

When a pawn reaches the other end it promotes to any piece — usually a Queen!

♟ En Passant

A special pawn capture. If a pawn moves two squares forward beside an enemy pawn, that pawn can capture it — but only immediately on the next move.
